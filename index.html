      Auto-sync from the audio

      A speech AI reads this song's audio file on your device and matches the words in it to your lyrics. Your music isn't uploaded anywhere.

      The song doesn't need to play. The AI reads the file directly, not through your speakers, so you can pause the music or play something else while it works. On a phone, pausing is best: it leaves more memory for the AI.

      The first time, it downloads the AI (about 80 MB, one time only) from Hugging Face and its engine from jsDelivr, then keeps them in this browser. Each song can take a few minutes. Some lines may need fixing afterward, especially in songs that mix languages.

      Auto-sync with AI needs a computer
      
    
      The speech AI needs more memory than phones give a web app, so on a phone the app closes partway through. It works on a computer:
      
    
        
      
      1. Open Clip & Play on a computer and add the same song.
        2. 
      
      2. Paste or import its lyrics, then choose Auto-sync with AI from the lyrics menu.
        3. 
      
      3. Choose Save as a .lrc file, and move that file to your phone, for example with AirDrop, email or iCloud Drive.
        4. 
      
      4. On your phone, open this song's lyrics and choose Import a .lrc file.
        5. 
    
      
    
      Or use Rough sync from the sound right here. It's quick and needs no AI, but its timing is an estimate, so expect to fix some lines.
